One dark mode with a dusk gradient

Left to right: today's Light and Dark, then two ways to give dark the same blue-to-warm idea as light. Each panel is a real page background with the same screen on it. Under each panel are the colours and whether the small grey text stays readable on them.

"Readable" means the muted grey text on a card keeps a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 at the top, the middle and the bottom of the page, which is the rule the app's tests already enforce. The cards are see-through, so their colour follows the page behind them.
