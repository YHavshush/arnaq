What changes, and what the ring means here

If you would rather the arc did something else (for example progress towards your savings goal when you have one), say so.
• The build-up lives on the page. One bar split into Rabobank, Revolut and Savings with the amounts. No link, no separate page. Tapping the bar could still open the detail with dates, but nothing needs it.
