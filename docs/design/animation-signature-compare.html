The signature, four ways

The pen is gone. Here are the turn you already know next to the other three ideas: the orbit, the split-flap and the light wipe. All of them start from the icon and end on the same finished word. Press "play all together" and watch them side by side.

A. the turn

The a and q roll apart

about 2 s
  • The a rolls left and the q right, each a full turn.
  • r, n and a are drawn between them.
  • Playful, the coin feel.
C. the orbit

The a and q circle each other

about 3 s
  • They orbit like two coins, faster and faster, with a little depth.
  • Then they are slung apart and the word draws in between.
  • The most energetic one.
D. the split-flap

Letters flip into place

about 2.5 s
  • The a and q slide apart.
  • r, n and a flip in like an old departure board, cycling through other letters first.
  • Mechanical and precise, which suits money.
E. the light wipe

A light sweeps the word in

about 2.5 s
  • The a and q slide apart.
  • A band of blue and purple light sweeps across and leaves the letters in ink behind it.
  • A glow on the q at the end. Calm and premium.

All four end as the same wordmark: ink letters and a gradient q, with a small pop. Nothing is built in the app yet. With "reduce motion" on, any of them shows the finished word.
