The signature, two ways

Both start from the icon and end on the same finished word. Play them one after the other or together and see which one feels more like Arnaq.

A. the turn

The a and q roll apart

about 2 s
  • The a rolls left and the q right, each a full turn.
  • r, n and a are drawn between them.
  • Energetic, a bit playful, the coin feel of the first idea.
B. the pen

A glowing pen writes the middle

about 3 s
  • The a and q slide apart without turning.
  • A point of light writes r, n and a, lifting between letters.
  • Calmer, more like a signature. A little longer.

Both end as the same wordmark: ink letters and a gradient q, with a small pop at the end. Nothing is built in the app yet. With "reduce motion" on, either one shows the finished word.
