The ring: net worth now

Today the ring on Ahead shows where your money ends in May. This mockup uses it for what you have right now. Your own numbers from the screenshot: Rabobank €357, Revolut €205, savings €201, BTW still owed −€168, so €595 now, and −€2,574 by May.

My take

It is a good idea, with one condition. A ring that says "now" on a page about "ahead" only works when the sentence next to it says what that means for the future. So the sentence becomes the bridge: "Your money lasts until December", and "−€2,574 by May" moves to the pill, where it is still one glance away. The May figure also stays at the end of the chart, so nothing is lost.

Option 1 is my pick, because it also removes the coloured bar: the ring itself is split by account, so Ahead has one element less.

Option 1 · The ring is split by account (my pick)

Each account is a part of the ring, in the same soft colours as the bar. The middle says what you have now. The BTW you owe is written under the number, since a ring cannot show a minus.

What it takes

The split ring already exists (it is the one on Fixed), so this is mostly the numbers: the ring gets the accounts as its parts, the bar goes, and the sentence gets the "lasts until" wording from the forecast's low points. The ring's colour would follow the sentence: calm when the money lasts beyond the horizon, warm when it does not.

One thing to decide: when your money does not run out, the sentence would say "Your money lasts beyond May" and the pill would show the growth, as it does now.
