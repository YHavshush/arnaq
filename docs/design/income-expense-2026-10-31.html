Income and expense: three ways to see how it is built up

Mockups with demo numbers, phone width, light and dark. All three can live together: 1 on Ahead, 2 on Fixed, 3 on Today. Each answers a different question.

1 · Ahead: tap a month, see what is inside the totals

Question it answers: "Where does this month's income and expense come from, and is it planned or already happened?" The three tiles stay. Tapping Income or Expenses opens the parts underneath. For a month that is still ahead every part says "expected"; for a month in progress each part also says how much is already paid.

2 · Fixed: a tracker, items down, months across

Question it answers: "Did every fixed item arrive or get paid, every month, and what do they add up to?" One cell per item and month: paid (green, with the real amount), late, missed (red) or still expected (grey). The column for this month is highlighted. Totals per month underneath. Scrolls sideways on a phone, the item names stay put.

3 · Today: where the month goes, in one bar

Question it answers: "Of what came in this month, what is already gone, to what, and what is left?" One bar equals the month's income. Segments are fixed costs, everyday spending, BTW set aside, savings, and what is left. When a month costs more than it brought in, the bar grows past the "came in" mark and the overspend is red. Under it, the last few months as small bars so you can compare.

My recommendation

Build 1 first (small: the tiles you already have gain a tap, and the data exists in the forecast). Then 2 (the tracker makes linking and finding mistakes much easier, and uses data the Fixed page already loads). 3 last, as it needs a clear rule for what counts as "everyday" and "saved" (so it matches the ring on Today). All three use the same colours as the app and the same few numbers, so they never disagree with each other.
