The plan grid: suggested, and yours to change

Categories down, months across. Every cell starts with a suggestion. Type over a cell and your number wins. Demo numbers, nothing in the app has changed.

How I would build it

  1. Three kinds of cell. A suggestion (grey, italic): your average or your budget, whichever you pick as the source. A number you typed (bold, with a dot). A computed cell (the BTW): it follows your income, so you do not type it.
  2. Fixed costs and income are editable too. They start from the Fixed page, and you can plan a change before it is real: rent goes up from March, income goes up from March. The cell shows "planned change". When it takes effect, you update the item on the Fixed page, and the cell then says "same as Fixed now" and offers a reset, so an old override never lingers.
  3. Tap a cell, see why. A suggestion says where it comes from: "average of Aug to Oct" or "your November budget". That answers "why is January so high" without guessing.
  4. Type once, choose how far it goes. After typing you pick "Only this month" (the default) or "This and the months after". The second is the fill-right of a spreadsheet, and it is also how a budget carries forward.
  5. Reset per cell. One tap puts the suggestion back. Nothing you typed is ever lost silently.
  6. Past months are read-only and show what really happened, so the grid reads as history on the left and plan on the right.
  7. The chart follows the grid. The totals at the bottom are exactly what the Ahead chart draws.

My pushback: a grid of 12 categories by 12 months is exactly the clutter you wanted gone. So by default it shows only the categories where you typed something plus your five biggest, and "Everything else" as one row. "Show all categories" opens the rest. It also lives one tap away from Ahead ("Edit the plan"), not on the page itself.

It replaces the "My own" button. A single everyday amount becomes "type it in the total row, and the months after follow". One control fewer on Ahead. Only the source (averages or budgets) stays, as the small "Based on" choice.

What it takes

• One new table with your typed amounts (a category, a fixed item or an income item; the month; the amount), your own rows only, like every other table. That is a hosted database change, so I will ask for your exact go-ahead before applying it.
• The forecast uses a typed cell first, then the suggestion or the Fixed page. Past months are never overwritten. The Fixed page itself never changes.
• The Ahead chart, the pills and the lowest-point marks follow the grid automatically.
• The "My own" basis goes away; its amount moves into the grid.

Decided, and what is still open

  1. Decided: "Edit the plan" is one tap from Ahead. Expected income is in the grid. After typing, the default is "Only this month". Fixed costs and income are editable.
  2. Open: when a planned change has really happened (the rent did go up), should the grid drop the override by itself once the Fixed page shows the same amount? I would, and show a short note "now same as Fixed" for a month so it does not vanish silently.
  3. Open: the BTW row follows income. Should you be able to type over it for a quarter where you know it will differ? I would say no, to keep it honest.
