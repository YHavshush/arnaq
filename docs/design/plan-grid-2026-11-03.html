The plan grid: suggested, and yours to change

Categories down, months across. Every cell starts with a suggestion. Type over a cell and your number wins. Demo numbers, nothing in the app has changed.

How I would build it

  1. Three kinds of cell. A suggestion (grey, italic): your average or your budget, whichever you pick as the source. A number you typed (bold, with a dot). A locked cell (padlock): fixed items and the BTW, which come from the Fixed page and cannot be typed over here.
  2. Tap a cell, see why. A suggestion says where it comes from: "average of Aug to Oct" or "your November budget". That answers "why is January so high" without guessing.
  3. Type once, choose how far it goes. After typing you pick "Only this month" or "This and the months after". The second is the fill-right of a spreadsheet, and it is also how a budget carries forward.
  4. Reset per cell. One tap puts the suggestion back. Nothing you typed is ever lost silently.
  5. Past months are read-only and show what really happened, so the grid reads as history on the left and plan on the right.
  6. The chart follows the grid. The totals at the bottom are exactly what the Ahead chart draws.

My pushback: a grid of 12 categories by 12 months is exactly the clutter you wanted gone. So by default it shows only the categories where you typed something plus your five biggest, and "Everything else" as one row. "Show all categories" opens the rest. It also lives one tap away from Ahead ("Edit the plan"), not on the page itself.

It replaces the "My own" button. A single everyday amount becomes "type it in the total row, and the months after follow". One control fewer on Ahead. Only the source (averages or budgets) stays, as the small "Based on" choice.

What I need from you
