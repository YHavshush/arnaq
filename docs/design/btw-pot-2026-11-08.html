Showing the BTW pot

The BTW money sits in a pot inside the Revolut savings account, and the bank file never names the pot. With one setting, arnaq counts your savings toward the BTW first, so the Ahead screen shows the BTW as covered. The net worth stays €595: nothing is counted twice. Invented numbers.

Ahead, before and after

Left: today, savings of €201 and a BTW of −€168 that looks unpaid. Next: the setting on. The savings show only what is left (€33), the pot gets its own orange slice and line, and the BTW line says "covered". The ring still adds up to the same total.

When the pot is smaller than the BTW owed

If the savings cannot cover the BTW, the line says how much is missing in red, so you can see it at a glance. Here €100 is set aside and €68 is short.

My BTW pot is part of my savings

Arnaq counts my savings toward the BTW first, so you can see it is covered. The savings goal then counts only what is left after the BTW.

One effect to know: the savings goal (today €201 of €1,000) would count only the €33 left after the BTW, so its progress looks lower. That is the honest figure, because the BTW money is not goal money.
