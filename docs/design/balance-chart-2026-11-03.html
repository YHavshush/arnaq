Balance chart: Ahead or a sparkline

The balance chart you asked about, shown two ways, on laptop and phone. Demo numbers, nothing in the app has changed. Both charts are drawn from the same example month so the scale is real.

My answer

Put the big chart on Ahead, and keep Fixed to the timeline list. Ahead is the page that answers "how long does my money last", and a line that starts at your real balance and runs through the next months answers that in one look. Fixed only has four events a month, so a big chart there is a flat line with steps.

I would skip the sparkline. The timeline already says the lowest point in red. A thin line next to it says the same thing a second time. If you like the extra touch, it is cheap and Option B shows how it would look.

One honest limit: past the fixed items, the line is a guess built from your averages, budgets or own amounts. That is why it is dashed, and why "Based on" stays under the chart.
