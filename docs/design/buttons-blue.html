One blue for everything

Each row is one candidate blue, used for the app's buttons and selected dots in light and dark, and for the onboarding's "Get Started". In light mode the selected radio dot is this same blue too: a blue disc with a small dark dot in the middle, so it stays visible on the white card (a plain pale dot would almost vanish). Text links like "Set budget" can't be this pale on a light page, so they stay navy. Dark mode keeps its ring and dot, now in the candidate blue.
