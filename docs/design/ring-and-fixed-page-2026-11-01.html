The two-tone ring, and ideas for the Fixed page

Mockups only, demo numbers, phone width. Nothing in the app has been changed.

A · Today: the ring shows spent and still-to-pay

The solid arc is what you have actually spent. The lighter arc is what is already spoken for: fixed costs still to pay. The big number is what is left over (budget − spent − still to pay), the same as now. The sentence says the same thing in words, so the ring and the text no longer seem to disagree. First of the month on the left (nothing paid yet), mid-month on the right.

The rules

• Fixed costs count as spent once they are paid (the arc turns from light to solid), and as to pay until then.
• Over budget: the arc turns red, as it does now, and the big number says how far over.
• The legend under the ring only shows two parts, so it stays one line on a phone.

B · The Fixed page: what is off about it now

Today the page repeats the same items three times, one under the other: the month's list, then "Manage" (pause, delete), then the month-by-month table. The totals sit as extra rows glued to the bottom of each list, "Left after fixed costs" is a separate card stuck to them, and suggestions and "missed" sit in between. So you scroll a long page and meet every fixed item again and again, in different shapes, with different corners. The elements are fine; the order and the repetition are the problem.

Idea 1: one item, one row. Every fixed item is a single row. It shows this month's state. Tap it and it opens to show its history (the months as dots) and its actions (pause, remove). That replaces the manage list and the table in one go. Totals move into the section headers, and one equation strip at the top says income − fixed = left.

What changes, and why

• The totals move up into the section header ("Expenses · €675 · 0 of 4 paid"). No glued extra rows, no separate "left" card: the equation strip carries it.
• The history is inside the row. The dots are the tracker, one item at a time. On a phone that is far easier to read than a table you scroll sideways.
• Suggestions get one small card at the top instead of two long sections; "Review" opens them.
• Missed items show as a red chip on the row itself, not a separate section.
• Every card has the same corners (24px) and the rows sit inside, so nothing is glued to anything.

What you lose: the all-items-by-all-months table. It can stay as a secondary view for the laptop ("By month" under the list), but I would drop it from the phone.

Idea 2: three tabs. Keep each view as it is, but show one at a time: This month, By month, Manage. Less change, no repetition on screen, but you have to tap to see the history, and "Manage" stays a separate list of the same items.

Which I would pick

Idea 1. It removes the repetition instead of hiding it, it keeps the page one scroll long, and it is the only one where "pause" and "remove" sit next to the item they act on. Idea 2 is the safer, smaller change if you want to keep the three views exactly as they are.

Either way I would also replace the glued "Total" rows with the header totals and give every box the same corners.
