Clear glass: how see-through can the bar be?

The selected item is the clear-glass bubble you chose. The bar gets progressively more see-through, from today's 38% fill down to almost nothing; the blur and colour lift go up as the fill goes down, so the content behind turns into soft colour instead of shapes. In dark the inactive labels are also a little brighter (#d3dae6 instead of #a8b1bf), which is what lets dark be this transparent and still readable. Under each bar there is a navy bar, a red bar and a green bar, the hardest things to read over. Each row also shows the measured contrast of the inactive labels on those real pixels (4.5 to 1 is the minimum we hold ourselves to).
