The control: what the coming months are based on

Averages (today's behaviour): fixed items plus the average of your last 3 months for everything else.
Budget: the fixed items plus what you budgeted. It answers "what happens if I keep to my budget?" and is usually the happier line.
My own: you type one number for everyday spending per month (and optionally income). It answers "what if I spend X?".
The same choice applies to income when you want: your fixed income, or your fixed income plus the average of other money that came in (like the €243 allowance each month).

The ring

The arc is the share of the coming months that end with more money than they started with (4 of 6 here, so 67%). It is a "how safe is this plan" gauge, not an amount. If you would rather not have an arc that needs explaining, the ring can be a plain full circle with the number inside, or show progress towards your savings goal when you have one.
