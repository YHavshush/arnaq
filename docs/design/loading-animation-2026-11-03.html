A calmer loading animation

The a and q circling each other goes away. These three keep the a and the q, stay in one place and only move a little. All three use only movement and fading, so they are safe on an iPhone, and all three stand still when the phone asks for less motion.

1 · Breathe (my pick)

The a swells and settles, then the q follows a moment later. Nothing travels, so it never looks busy, and it still looks right when it plays for only a fraction of a second.

2 · Draw

Each letter is drawn with one line, the a first and then the q. It is the closest to how the logo is built, but a short wait shows only half a drawing.

3 · Hop

The a lifts a little and settles, then the q, like a slow wave. It is the liveliest of the three and the most playful.

Where it appears: only on the page-loading placeholder (the big letters above the grey bars) and anywhere else that waits with no known length. The small spinner in buttons, the finished tick and the first-open animation stay as they are.
