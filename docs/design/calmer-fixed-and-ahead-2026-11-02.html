Calmer: Fixed and Ahead

Answers first, then three layouts for Fixed and a calmer Ahead. Demo numbers, nothing in the app has changed.

Is the graph worth it? My honest answer

For the Fixed page, no. It only draws your fixed items, so it is a flat line with two or three steps. That is not much to look at, and it takes the most room on the page. What is useful in it is two facts: when money arrives and leaves and the lowest point. A short list says both better (date, what, amount, balance after, lowest in red), and it is easier to read on a phone. So: drop the graph, keep a timeline list. (The Ahead chart is different: it has a real shape over many months, so it stays.)

Why it sat next to the list: I put the ring in a left column and everything else on the right so a laptop screen would not be empty on the sides. It ended up looking like two separate pages. A single centred column (like the phone) is calmer and is what most budget apps do on a laptop too.

F-C · if you would rather keep a graph

Then make it earn its place: put it on Ahead instead, where it can show the real balance over the next months, and let Fixed only show the timeline. Or keep a very small sparkline (a thin line, no labels) in the timeline card header. I would not keep the big version.

The table you had before (items down, months across) is still in the app's history. It is good for checking months (did everything come in?) and bad as a first impression. If you miss it, it can come back as a "Months" view behind one tap, below the lists.

What I took out and why

• The italic month names sentence becomes one plain line that says the thing that matters (the direction and the size).
• The "0 of 7 months end in the plus" note goes: the pill already says it, and the ring arc stays as a quiet gauge. (If you want, the ring can lose its arc entirely and be a plain circle.)
• One row of controls in the chart card header (the horizon), and the basis becomes a small "Based on: your averages ▾" choice at the bottom of the card instead of a second toggle row and a sentence. It still switches in one tap.
• The chart's own legend sentence goes (the dashes are already explained once by the word "forecast" on the months).
• The bar sits next to the ring's text, right-aligned total underneath, with no title.
• The BTW card and the month list stay below, unchanged.
