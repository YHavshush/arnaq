Five names, five logo sketches

The app's ring becomes part of the name: the Q in Qesef and Qianbao, the q in arnaq, a crossing of two arcs for the x in Arnax and Mezumax. Same colours as the app, in the light and dark theme. These are first sketches to judge the names by sight, not final logos.

My read

Sounds best: arnaq (two crisp syllables, ar-NAK) and Mezumax (meh-ZOO-max, rolls off the tongue). Safest to own: Mezumax, the only one with a free .com and an easy spelling. Weakest: Qianbao, because English speakers will not know how to say it and several Chinese finance apps already use the word.
