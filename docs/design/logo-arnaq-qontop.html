The purple q on top of the blue a

The original pair, the blue a and the purple q, now laid over each other with the q on top. Exactly on top the q would hide the a completely, so the a peeks out behind it: to the left (and a little down in one). The rings keep an empty middle, the a has its short stem, the q its long one. Wordmark first, then as an app icon at three sizes.
