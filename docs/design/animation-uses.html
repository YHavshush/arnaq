Where each animation goes

I picked the uses so you don't have to. The one you like in the onboarding stays there. The a and q turning and the word coming out of the logo becomes the signature animation for opening the app. Then a small family of loading animations built from the same ring and stem. The basic ones from the first shortlist are gone.

The signature: aq turns, arnaq comes out

The a rolls left and the q rolls right, each turning a full circle, while r, n and a are drawn between them. The a turns from blue to ink and the q gets its gradient. The word starts and ends with the same two letters as the icon, so the logo literally opens into the name.

Plays once when the app opens or the login page loads, about 2 seconds in total, then fades into the screen. Not on every page, and shown as the finished word when the device asks for reduced motion.

The loading family

Five small ones, all made from the logo's ring and stem, so waiting also feels like Arnaq. Each has one job.

chase

quick waits, buttons, page changes

The a's ring with a gradient arc running round it. Small and quiet.

swap

longer waits with no known length

The a and q trade places and stay upright. The logo itself, waiting.

0%

fill

reading a statement

The app's own ring, filling with the real progress, then a tick. Honest, because it shows how far along it is.

shimmer

lists and cards while they load

Placeholder lines with a blue highlight sweeping across. Standard, cheap and recognisable.

done

saved, imported, goal reached

The ring closes in green and the tick draws itself. Plays once.
