What I might take from KokonutUI

None of this is built, and I would not install KokonutUI itself (it brings its own look and an animation library). These are the ideas in it that fit arnaq, drawn in arnaq's own look with the app's colours. Say yes or no to each one. Some of them animate here so you can see the movement; in the app they would play once and respect "reduce motion".

1 · Numbers that count up
Net worth by April
€03,480+€1,170

The big numbers (ring centre, net worth, "left over") roll up to their value when the page opens, once. It makes a screen feel alive and it draws the eye to the one number that matters. Where: Today ring, Ahead ring, Fixed ring.

2 · Slide to confirm, instead of "tap twice"
Remove “Netflix”?
→Slide to remove

Removing a fixed item or erasing data today asks you to tap Remove and then Confirm. A slider is harder to hit by accident and feels deliberate. Where: Remove on a Fixed row, Erase data in Settings, restoring a backup.

3 · A tank for the savings goal
62%of €5,000 goal
€3,100 saved

The savings goal as a slowly moving tank instead of a bar. Pretty, but only worth it if you set a goal. Where: the Savings row on Today (small) and the Budget page's savings card.

4 · A bento layout for the top of Ahead (laptop)
Net worth by April€3,480+€1,170 over 6 months
Income€1,468
Expenses€1,273

Unequal tiles instead of one row of equal boxes, which would answer "it looks like one block under another". Probably only for the laptop. Where: the Ahead top, possibly Today.

5 · Three rings in one (activity card)
Spent 54% · Saved 60% · Fixed still to pay 32%

One compact graphic that shows three things at once. It replaces nothing today; it would be a small extra on Today under the ring (spent, saved, fixed still to pay). Risk: three rings are harder to read than one.

What I would pick

1 (count-up) and 2 (slide to confirm): low risk, add polish and safety, and both use Motion's small animate() and a pointer drag, no new look. 4 (bento) for the laptop top of Ahead, if you still find it too stacked after the new layout. 3 and 5 only if you want them; they are pretty but add things to read.

If you say yes to some, I will build them with Motion (already installed) in arnaq's tokens, test them on a phone size, and keep them off when the device asks for less motion.
