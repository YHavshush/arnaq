Fixed: a calmer top. Ahead: without the ring.

Mockups only, demo numbers, phone width. Nothing in the app has been changed. The list under the top of the Fixed page is Idea 1 from before (one row per item, tap to open) and is not repeated here.

1 · Fixed: the top of the page

What bothers me about the current top too: a ring that says "5/5 paid" (a ring for a count), three little cards for a sum, and then a card of suggestions, all in different shapes. Three calmer alternatives. None of them uses a card or a ring; they sit straight on the page background like the Today header does, and the same three numbers are all there (comes in, fixed, left).

My pick: F1. One number you care about ("what is left once the fixed things are covered"), one bar that shows where the income goes, and a single quiet line for progress. F2 puts progress first, which makes the page about ticking bills off. F3 is the most balanced but the least "answer first". Suggestions would be a single line under the header (a small link "2 suggestions"), not a card.

2 · Ahead without the ring

The ring on Ahead shows a net worth in its centre and a vague "share of months in the plus" around it, which is what felt strange. Without it, the top is just the answer in words and numbers, and the chart becomes the hero. Three ideas, all with the same chart, month detail and BTW card underneath as now.

My pick: A3 with A1's sentence kept under the chart. The number and the chart are one thing: you touch a month and the big number shows that month's net worth, then its income, expenses and growth tiles open right below (the tiles you already have, and the breakdown on tap). It removes the question "what does the top number mean?" because it always belongs to the month you are looking at. A1 is the safest if you want to keep a fixed headline ("by April") that never moves.

A message about a negative future (net worth falling) uses the same layout: the pill turns red ("−€1,759 a month") and the sentence says why in plain words, no colour change of a whole ring.
