Ring colours

The rings with more than one arc (Fixed, and Ahead now that it shows where your money is) use five colours that do not belong to each other: cyan, periwinkle, green, orange and grey. Here are four ways to colour them from what the app already has. Every ring follows the ring rules: gradient arcs with round caps and no glow in light, a dim-to-lit arc with a lit end and a tight glow in dark, and flat slivers for very small shares. Demo numbers.

My pick is B, the chip colours. The sky, lavender, mint and peach of the icon chips and tiles are already how the app says "this kind of thing". Rabobank, Revolut and savings would be the colours they had in the bar you liked, and the dark ring would use the chip colours the app already has for dark.
