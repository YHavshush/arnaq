My honest view

Today already has the ring, the totals line, the category list and the Pace card, and most of what this bar says is repeated there in another form: fixed and everyday spending are in the Pace card, what is left is the ring. What the bar adds is the single picture of income against everything it was used for (including BTW and saved), and the comparison with earlier months. If that is not something you reach for, the better home for it is not Today at all but the Ahead page month detail (idea 1), which already shows exactly these parts when you tap Income and Expenses. I would skip it on Today and keep Today calm.

If you still want it, this placement works: full width under the headline on a phone and a laptop, no extra navigation, about 90 px tall on a phone and 110 px on a laptop.
