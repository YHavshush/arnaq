The Fixed page hero: four ideas that earn their place

The job of this top: say what your income and fixed costs leave over, at a glance, and be a bit more than three numbers. Each idea below adds something you cannot see in the list underneath. Demo numbers, phone width. Nothing in the app has changed.

1 · The ring, on income · light

The same ring language as Today. The ring is your income: solid = fixed costs already paid, lighter = still to pay, the empty rest = what is left. One number in the middle.

The same ring, but each fixed item is its own colour, so you see what your fixed costs are made of. Paid items are solid, items still to come are lighter. Tap the legend to jump to the item.

Adds the thing the list cannot show: when it comes in and goes out, day by day. It tells you whether the fixed items are covered at every moment of the month, not only at the end. With your real balance it shows your actual lowest point.

4 · Written out, with a measure · dark

The mark is 50%, the usual advice for fixed costs. You are under it.

The most like the Today header: one sentence in the app's voice. The bar and the 50% mark give it a judgement (are my fixed costs high?) instead of just numbers.

What I would do

Idea 2 as the hero (the ring split by item, with "€793 left over" in the middle): it is the ring you liked, it matches Today, and the colours tell you what your fixed costs are made of. Idea 3 right under it as a card called "The month, day by day", because "am I short before the income arrives?" is the one thing a fixed-cost page can answer that no other page does. Idea 4's 50% mark could go into the sentence of idea 2 as a small bonus line.
