Arnaq: names for the features, and how the first minute could feel

You asked me to take the other AI's version into account, check all the options, and add my own. Below: what I would keep from it, what I would change and why, five naming sets, and three onboarding flows with mock screens. Nothing is built yet. You choose.

1. The pasted version, read honestly

Worth keeping

  • Three screens, short, one idea each. That matches what the research on onboarding shows (a short carousel, then quickly to first value).
  • Lowercase, quiet headlines. It fits the calm tone already in the app ("Your money, one calm number at a time").
  • One strong, simple button per screen, in the brand blue.
  • Giving the features a voice instead of "module" words.

I would change

  • "AI-driven pulse", "intelligent caps", "autopilot", "effortless" promise things Arnaq does not do. It reads uploaded statements (no bank link), and the sorting learns from your choices, it is not AI. A budgeting app lives on trust, so I would not claim it.
  • "Vaults" is Revolut's word for its savings goals, and you use Revolut. People would think Arnaq is a Revolut add-on.
  • "Caps" is ambiguous (limit? capital letters? a hat?) and has no natural Dutch word.
  • "Flow" and "pulse" are used by many apps and say nothing concrete. A new user cannot guess what is behind them.

My recommendation

Use C (lowercase plain) for the menu and headlines: it takes the best part of the pasted version, its calm lowercase voice, and drops the parts that are vague or borrowed. Put the personality in the words around the features (onboarding, empty states, the loading screen) rather than in the menu labels, because menu labels must be understood in half a second. If you prefer a more cautious step, F changes only one word.

Either way, the pasted feature names stay out of the app; they can live on as marketing words later if you like, once the Revolut collision is considered.

5. Onboarding flow 3: no screens, a checklist

The app opens straight on the overview, empty but friendly, with a small checklist that ticks itself.

overview
welcome to arnaq three things to get your number:
◻ add a statement◻ look at your fixed items◻ set a savings goal (optional)

Why consider it

  • Nothing to skip: the app is the tutorial.
  • Works for returning users who log in on a new phone.
  • Cheapest to build, and it doubles as the empty state.

Why not first choice

  • No "moment". A first-time user sees empty screens before they see value.
  • No place for the logo animation as a hook.

6. My own ideas, any flow can take these

Sample data mode

A button that fills a throwaway account with invented payments, so people can try before uploading anything. Also useful for your user testing with 5 to 8 people.

A privacy line at the upload step

"No bank login. Your statement is read, sorted and kept in your own account. Export or delete anytime." It is true today, and it is the thing that makes people comfortable uploading.

The logo animation as the only decoration

Use the animation you pick (rolling coin, the stem joins, ripple) once, on the welcome screen and while the first statement is being read. Never again after that.

Dutch and English from the first screen

Pick the language from the phone, with a small switch on the welcome screen. All text goes through the catalogs, so both are done in one pass.
